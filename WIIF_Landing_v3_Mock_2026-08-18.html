The economic-gravity map
1 · Substitute

What AI can reproduce
Cognition itself, and the layers built on selling it.
Value leaves. Prices fall toward the cost of compute.
  • Routine analysis
  • Content production
  • Back office / BPO
  • Per-seat software
2 · Amplify

What AI consumes more of
The complements cheap intelligence needs more of.
Demand rises. The bottleneck earns the rent.
  • Energy & grid
  • Cooling & data centers
  • Physical actuation
  • Agent rails & verification
3 · Reprice

What AI can't create
The non-reproducible. Intelligence cannot print more.
Scarcity re-rates. Surplus flows to the fixed.
  • Land & prime space
  • Authentic experience
  • Scarce rights / access
  • Human attention
4 · Unlock

What AI makes viable
Markets that were uneconomic until cognition got cheap.
Categories appear. New supply, new demand.
  • Long-tail markets
  • Cost-collapse services
  • Continuous systems
  • Abundance-native primitives

Version one of the map. It changes when the evidence changes.

Research

A living map of the post-AI economy

When intelligence collapses in price, value doesn't stay in the intelligence layer. It leaves what can be reproduced, flows to the complements and the non-reproducible, and pools in categories that only become viable once cognition is nearly free. The essays follow it, with named companies, real figures, and the conditions that would make the argument wrong.

The time path Buildout · Digestion · Diffusion
Phase I · now

Buildout

≈ 2025–27
What happens
The physical substrate goes up faster than it can be supplied. Power, grid, cooling and silicon become the binding constraints, and the constraint sets the price.
Where the value sits
With whoever owns the bottleneck. Scarcity earns the rent, not the software.
Rhymes with
  • Canal Mania, Britain1790–93
  • US railroad boom1866–73
  • Long-haul fibre buildout1996–2000
Phase II · next

Digestion

≈ 2027–30
What happens
The question turns from "can it be built" to "did it pay." Returns on the buildout are tested in public and financing gets selective. This is where the reset lives.
Where the value sits
With the survivors, and with whoever still holds cash. Most of the durable positions of the next decade are bought here.
Rhymes with
  • Canal share collapse1793–97
  • Railroad panic, then six lean years1873–79
  • Telecom bust2000–02
Phase III · later

Diffusion

≈ 2030–35
What happens
The capability stops being the story and becomes the substrate. It shows up in ordinary companies' margins rather than in vendors' revenue.
Where the value sits
With rails, trust, land and attention, and with the categories that exist only because cognition got cheap.
Rhymes with
  • Cheap bulk transport feeds the factory economy
  • National distribution, then mail-order retail
  • Dark fibre becomes broadband, cloud, streaming

Each phase pays a different owner. Getting the sequence wrong is the most expensive way to be right about the destination.

What the essays go after
  1. Which complements cheap intelligence consumes, and how long their scarcity actually holds before supply responds.
  2. What intelligence cannot reproduce, and what happens to the price of it when everything else gets cheap.
  3. Which businesses only become possible once cognition is nearly free, and which of them can hold a moat.
  4. Where control survives the collapse, and where the moat is already gone and the market hasn't noticed.
  5. What would make all of this wrong: a capability plateau, adoption that stalls, or the labs capturing the complements themselves.

The order changes as the evidence arrives. The questions are the fixed part.

Investments

The thesis, held as positions

A concentrated global strategy built on one rule: own what stays scarce when intelligence becomes abundant. Capital goes to the complements the buildout consumes, what it cannot reproduce, and the categories it makes possible.

  • Conviction on the durable layer. Power, grid, cooling and actuation, owned now and sized to hold through the cycle.
  • Discipline on the depreciating layer. Frontier silicon and platforms are bought on drawdowns, never on the narrative.
  • Reserve is a position. Cash and an options overlay are held to buy the reset the strategy expects, not to sit out the market.

Advisory

Turn the AI shift into a clearer strategic choice

I work with founders, companies and capital providers on decisions that sit between technology, market structure, product and capital: where value is moving, what becomes newly viable, where defensibility survives, and what to prioritize now.

AI-native foundersCompanies in transitionProduct & strategy leadersInvestors & boards
Questions worth working on
Which parts of our market are being commoditized — and where does value re-pool?
Map the donor set, the complements, and the position most likely to capture the shift.
What becomes possible now that cognition is cheap?
Find categories, service models and product frequencies that were previously uneconomic.
Where is our defensibility strengthening — and where is it quietly eroding?
Test data, workflow, distribution, trust, permission and network advantages against the new economics.
Which bets deserve capital now, later, or not at all?
Separate buildable from ownable, sequence the choices, and name the evidence needed to commit.
